Pick your stretch of river

Three signature trips and a few extras. Every trip includes a certified guide and all safety gear.

Gentle Float

Half day, Class II, ages 6 and up

Our friendliest trip. Ease into the sport on splashy, forgiving rapids separated by long calm pools where you can swim, spot herons and take photos. Guides teach paddle commands before launch, so no experience is needed.

Best for families, first-timers and anyone who wants fun without the nerves.

Canyon Day Run

Full day, Class III, ages 10 and up

Eight hours on the water through a scenic canyon with steady Class III rapids. You will paddle together through waves and drops, stop for a hot riverside lunch, and have time for a cliff-side swim.

Best for active groups who want a real adventure with a safety net of expert guides.

Thunder Gorge

Full day, Class IV, ages 16 and up

Big water for experienced paddlers. Expect steep drops, powerful holes and technical lines that demand teamwork. A short safety talk and practice session come first, and trips run only when river levels allow.

Best for athletic adults who have rafted before and want a challenge.
