Why we run the river

Ridgeline Rapids exists to get people onto moving water safely and with a big grin. Our certified guides brief every group, fit every helmet and life jacket, and read the river so you can focus on paddling.

Whether this is your first raft or your fiftieth, there is a stretch of river that matches you.

What we offer

  • Half-day trips for families and first-timers.
  • Full-day adventures with a riverside lunch.
  • Advanced runs for experienced paddlers.
  • Group and school outings with custom schedules.

All gear is included: raft, paddle, helmet, life jacket, and wetsuit.
